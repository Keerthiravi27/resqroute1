Numbers show the ranking. Red dot = ambulance. Distances are straight-line, not road distance.

How the points work

Maximum 100: hospital available 10, general bed 10, specialist 30, equipment 30, ICU bed 10 (only counted when the request is Critical), distance 10 (0 km = 10 points, 20 km or more = 0). The label (Meets stated needs / Missing a requirement / Unavailable) is applied first, and points only order hospitals inside the same label. A close hospital can never outrank a hospital that has everything the request needs.
